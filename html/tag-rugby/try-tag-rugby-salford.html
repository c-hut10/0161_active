About the club

Try Tag Rugby Salford offers fun, inclusive and social mixed-gender tag rugby leagues for adults aged 18+ across Salford and East Manchester. The game is suitable for beginner or experienced players and everyone is welcome! Our friendly leagues are a great way to get active, meet new people and enjoy being part of a community. We offer free taster sessions so you can give tag rugby a go before committing. No experience necessary, just come along and give it a try!

All welcome. Come down and give it a try! Taster sessions are FREE. Contact the club for regular pricing. The cost to join a league can be found on our website and varies between each league.
