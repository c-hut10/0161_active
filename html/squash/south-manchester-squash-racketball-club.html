About the club

We are a community run Squash and a Racketball club based in Manchester (UK). We offer sessions for Squash and Racketball at Hough End Leisure Centre, on a membership as well as pay-per-play basis. We welcome players (18 years upwards) at all levels for both sports, from beginners to advanced, all genders and abilities. Equipment is provided by the club.
