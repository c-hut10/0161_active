# Cookie notice and settings

Every full page loads `js/cookie-settings.js` and `css/cookie-settings.css`. The footer provides a privacy-page fallback link when JavaScript is unavailable.

There are currently no optional tracking services or consent toggles. The panel is a storage notice and acknowledgement controller, not consent to future tracking. Choosing “Got it” stores `0161active-cookie-notice` in local storage with a version and timestamp, valid for 90 days. No record is sent to the server. Expired records are deleted on the next visit. Reset removes the record and shows the banner again. Storage failures leave the website usable.

Before adding analytics, advertising or other optional storage, inventory each service, add specific default-off choices, enforce those choices before loading the service, implement withdrawal and cleanup, update the privacy policy and increment the notice version. Do not treat acknowledgement of the current notice as consent for a new purpose.
