About 0161 Active

Hi there,

My name is Cian. I'm building 0161 Active to help people discover sports clubs across Manchester. A free club profile brings your training times, location, costs and contact details together, so newcomers can understand what you offer and how to get involved - I've found that taster sessions are crucial for attracting new interest.

Who can join

Beginners welcome?

A lot of the clubs listed on the site are welcoming beginners. If, however, your club is playing at a competitive level, there is a chance to detail this in your club profile.
