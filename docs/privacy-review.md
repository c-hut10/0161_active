# Privacy policy review and retention operations

Owner supplied on 4 October 2026: Cian Hutton owns 0161_Active. For an unsuccessful application, give the club 48 hours’ notice to make the amendments required for listing. If the requirements are not met by the deadline, delete its application information and associated personal data.

The draft at html/privacy.html records that rule and describes services evidenced in the repository: Netlify hosting and Forms, the contact Gmail mailbox, a local-storage acknowledgement for the cookie notice (valid for 90 days). The map is paused and does not request tiles or libraries. The registration form now requests Netlify-provided Google reCAPTCHA at deployment. Its security cookie and processing are disclosed in the policy and cookie panel; review the actual deployed provider behaviour and agreements. There is no newly introduced contact form or analytics integration.

Before publishing the policy as final, confirm:

- The lawful basis for enquiries, application processing and publication of personal club contact details; document any legitimate-interest assessment if applicable.
- Retention for general enquiries and service-provider records. Successful club details follow docs/retention-schedule.md.
- Current Netlify and Google account settings, processing agreements, subprocessors and international transfer arrangements; review map-provider policies before restoring the map.
- How the 48-hour deletion rule will be performed: record the notice, amendments required and 48-hour deadline, delete the submission from Netlify Forms and associated application correspondence from the mailbox, and check spam/deleted-item folders and attachments. Handle provider backup retention separately and accurately describe any limits.

The site does not automate deletions. A privacy-page promise is not an operational deletion mechanism. The unsuccessful-application notice period begins when the notice is sent. Review amendments received during that period; delete the application information and associated personal data after the period if it still does not qualify.

Source for required privacy information: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/the-right-to-be-informed/what-privacy-information-should-we-provide/

Successful club details are retained while the club consents to inclusion. Removal requests must be actioned within 48 hours of receipt. Record consent and withdrawals; do not assume public-source research or an existing listing establishes consent. See retention-schedule.md.
