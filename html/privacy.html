YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy.

How information is handled when you contact 0161 Active, submit a club application or browse our directory.

Draft for review. The lawful bases and provider processing and international transfer arrangements need confirmation before this policy is published.

Who is responsible?

The data controller is Cian Hutton, operating as 0161_Active.

For questions about your information or a request to change or remove personal details, email contact.0161active@gmail.com.

Club applications and enquiries

The club registration form asks for club details, location, session information, pricing, a contact email and a website or social link. Applications are stored through Netlify Forms and reviewed manually. Emails you send are handled through our Gmail mailbox.

We use these details to review applications, respond to enquiries and maintain the directory. Approved club details, public meeting points and contact information may appear on the website, as explained on the registration form. Tell us if your contact email should not be published. Avoid sending private home addresses or sensitive personal information.

Information from public sources

Some directory information comes from club websites and other public sources recorded in our research data. Profiles distinguish public-source checks from direct club confirmation. Contact us if a listing includes outdated or inappropriate personal details.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not currently use optional analytics or advertising cookies. The published registration form uses Google reCAPTCHA, provided through Netlify, to help prevent automated spam. The security check may process your IP address, browser and device information, and interactions with the check, and may set the _GRECAPTCHA cookie for risk analysis. If you cannot use the check, contact us by email instead.

Our cookie settings control uses local storage to remember when you acknowledge the notice. The record contains a notice version and the time of your choice; it is used for 90 days and is not sent to us. An expired record is removed when you next visit.

Accessibility settings also use local storage to remember your larger-text and reduced-motion preferences on this device until you change them or clear browser site data. These preferences are not sent to us.

Open “Cookie settings” in any page’s footer to review this information or choose “Forget saved choice”. You can also clear the record through your browser’s site-data settings. If storage is blocked, the notice may appear again on your next visit.

Netlify hosts this website and processes form submissions. Requests to the hosting provider may include your IP address and browser information; provider logs are separate from browser storage. The map is currently paused and does not load map software or tiles. If we introduce optional tracking, we will update this policy and provide the relevant choices before it starts.

Retention schedule

Successful club applications and listings

We retain successful clubs’ details for as long as the club consents to being included on the 0161 Active website. A club can withdraw that consent by emailing contact.0161active@gmail.com. We will take the listing down within 48 hours of receiving notice.

When consent is withdrawn, the club’s details will no longer be retained for inclusion in the directory. Removal from our website does not automatically remove copies already held by search engines or other third parties.

Unsuccessful club applications

If a club application does not meet our listing requirements, we will give the club 48 hours’ notice before deleting its information. During this period, the club can make the amendments needed to qualify for inclusion on the website. If the requirements have not been met by the end of the notice period, we will delete the application information and associated personal data, including copies held in our application system and mailbox. Applications that qualify will follow the successful-club retention schedule.

Other enquiries and service-provider records

Retention arrangements for other enquiries and provider records are still being confirmed.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or erase your personal information, restrict its use, receive a copy or object to its use. Email us to raise a request. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office.
