YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy.

How information is handled when you contact 0161 Active, submit a club application or browse our directory.

Draft for review. The lawful bases and provider processing and international transfer arrangements need confirmation before this policy is published.

Who is responsible?

The data controller is Cian Hutton, operating as 0161_Active.

For questions about your information or a request to change or remove personal details, email contact.0161active@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

We collect club details, training addresses and postcodes, session information, pricing, contact email addresses, online links and any additional information you supply. We use this information to review applications, maintain the directory and respond to enquiries.

Approved club details, training locations, contact emails and additional information may be published on your club profile. Tell us before publication if your contact email should remain private. Do not include private home addresses or sensitive personal information in public club details.

We also use information from club websites and other public sources. Contact us if a listing contains outdated or inappropriate personal information.

Service providers and overseas processing

Netlify hosts the website and stores form submissions. Google provides our Gmail mailbox and the reCAPTCHA security check used through Netlify. These services handle information needed to deliver the website, process applications, manage correspondence and prevent spam. Website requests may include your IP address and browser information.

Our providers may process personal information outside the UK, including in the United States. Netlify's Data Processing Agreement provides for the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and contractual safeguards where applicable. The transfer safeguards and processing terms applicable to our Google services and provider accounts are still being confirmed before this policy is finalised.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not currently use optional analytics or advertising cookies. Google reCAPTCHA helps prevent automated spam. It may process your IP address, browser and device information, and interactions with the check, and may set a security cookie. If you cannot use the check, contact us by email instead.

We store your acknowledgement of the cookie notice on your device for 90 days, including the notice version and the time it was acknowledged. We also remember your larger-text and reduced-motion preferences until you change them or clear browser storage. These preferences are not sent to us.

You can review the notice or forget its saved acknowledgement through “Cookie settings” in the footer. You can also remove saved information through your browser's site-data settings. Acknowledging the notice does not switch reCAPTCHA on or off.

Retention schedule

Successful club applications and listings

We retain successful clubs’ details for as long as the club consents to being included on the 0161 Active website. A club can withdraw that consent by emailing contact.0161active@gmail.com. We will take the listing down within 48 hours of receiving notice.

When consent is withdrawn, the club’s details will no longer be retained for inclusion in the directory. Removal from our website does not automatically remove copies already held by search engines or other third parties.

Unsuccessful club applications

If a club application does not meet our listing requirements, we will give the club 48 hours’ notice before deleting its information. During this period, the club can make the amendments needed to qualify for inclusion on the website. If the requirements have not been met by the end of the notice period, we will delete the application information and associated personal data, including copies held in our application system and mailbox. Applications that qualify will follow the successful-club retention schedule.

Other enquiries and service-provider records

Retention arrangements for other enquiries and provider records are still being confirmed.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or erase your personal information, restrict its use, receive a portable copy, or object to its use, where these rights apply under data protection law.

To make a request, email contact.0161active@gmail.com. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office.
